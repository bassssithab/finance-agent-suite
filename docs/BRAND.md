# Brand

## Brand architecture: branded house

One master brand; agents are named functionally underneath it.

```
LedgerMind                        ← platform brand (working name)
├── LedgerMind Reconcile
├── LedgerMind Close
├── LedgerMind Audit Ready
├── LedgerMind Tax
├── LedgerMind Controls
└── ...
```

Why a branded house (vs. separate product brands):
- Every agent sold strengthens trust in the next one — cross-sell is the
  whole economic model of a suite
- Finance buyers are risk-averse; one credible brand beats twelve unknowns
- Marketing budget concentrates instead of fragmenting

## Naming criteria
- Credible to a CFO/Controller (no cute misspellings)
- Hints at finance/ledger/assurance without being generic ("FinanceAI")
- .com or .ai available; trademark-clearable in US/EU/GCC

## Positioning statement (draft)
For finance and compliance teams drowning in manual, evidence-heavy work,
**LedgerMind** is a suite of AI agents that do the preparing while your team
does the approving — with an audit trail behind every action. Unlike point
tools or generic copilots, every LedgerMind agent runs on one shared,
auditor-grade platform.
